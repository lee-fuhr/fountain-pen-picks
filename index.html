How we choose our picks

We don’t claim to test every pen ourselves. Instead, we comb through long-term owner feedback, published reviews from trusted sources, and discussions in fountain pen communities. We pay close attention to what actually holds up over years of use, nib tines that stay aligned, converters that don’t crack, feeds that keep ink flowing. Pens that earn consistent praise and avoid common failure points rise to the top. Our picks are also informed by the reputation of the manufacturers themselves. We look for brands with a track record of good quality control and accessible spare parts. If a pen is frequently recommended by enthusiasts who’ve owned it for years, and if those recommendations hold across different inks and paper, we consider it a reliable choice. We prioritize pens that offer a genuine writing experience at a fair price.
